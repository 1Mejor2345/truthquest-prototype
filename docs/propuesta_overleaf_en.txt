\section{Executive Summary}

\begin{keybox}
\textbf{TruthQuest} is a gamified mobile web application that turns Media and Information Literacy (MIL) into a competitive and social gaming experience. Users learn to detect disinformation, AI-generated content, bias, clickbait, and hate speech through micro-challenges with immediate educational feedback, real-time 1v1 duels, and collaborative community missions.
\end{keybox}

\noindent The educational core is \textbf{100\% free}, guaranteeing equity and inclusion regardless of the user's socioeconomic background. Monetization is based exclusively on optional visual cosmetics, creating zero barriers to learning.

TruthQuest transforms the user from a passive consumer of information into a \textbf{"Digital Sage"}: an active agent who identifies, questions, and debunks disinformation both in the app and in their daily life.

\section{Problem Statement}

\subsection{The Global Context}

According to a global UNESCO survey (2023), \textbf{85\% of people} are concerned about the impact of online disinformation, and 87\% believe it has already had a significant effect on the political life of their countries. Youth aged 14 to 25—despite being digital natives—massively lack practical tools to discern fact from fiction in an ecosystem saturated with:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5cm, itemsep=4pt]
  \item[\textcolor{TQrose}{\faIcon{robot}}] \textbf{AI-Generated Content} — deepfakes, synthetic images, automated texts.
  \item[\textcolor{TQrose}{\faIcon{bullhorn}}] \textbf{Viral Disinformation} — fake news that spreads 6 times faster than the truth (MIT, 2018).
  \item[\textcolor{TQrose}{\faIcon{comments}}] \textbf{Hate Speech \& Polarization} — echo chambers that reinforce cognitive biases.
  \item[\textcolor{TQrose}{\faIcon{ad}}] \textbf{Clickbait \& Emotional Manipulation} — headlines designed to provoke reactions, not to inform.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{The Educational Gap}

Existing media education programs are mostly \textbf{theoretical, academic, and unappealing} to new generations. The result: low retention rates and almost zero impact on the young person's actual behavior when browsing social networks.

\begin{warnbox}
\textbf{Key Fact:} 86\% of global internet users admit to having been fooled by fake news at least once (CIGI-Ipsos, 2019). Among 16-24 year olds, continuous exposure to visual platforms makes them especially vulnerable to synthetic content and deepfakes.
\end{warnbox}

\subsection{Universal Accessibility: Breaking the Language Barrier}

A truly global MIL solution must transcend language and visual limitations. Aligning closely with UNESCO's mandate for universal education, TruthQuest integrates two groundbreaking accessibility pillars directly into its core architecture:

\vspace{6pt}
\noindent
\begin{minipage}[t]{0.6\textwidth}
  \vspace{0pt}
  \begin{accentbox}
  \textbf{\faIcon{universal-access}\; The Six Official UNESCO Languages \& Real-Time TTS}
  \begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1cm, itemsep=2pt, topsep=4pt]
    \item \textbf{Global Translation Engine:} With a single tap, the entire application interface translates instantly into the six official UNESCO languages: \textbf{English, French, Spanish, Arabic, Chinese, and Russian}. Our dynamic React DOM is engineered to maintain perfect layout integrity across all alphabets, including Right-to-Left (RTL) scripts.
    \item \textbf{Immersive Text-to-Speech (TTS):} An integrated Web Audio API synthesizer allows visually impaired users, or auditory learners, to hover over or tap any text on the screen to have the content read aloud. 
    \item \textbf{Zero-Latency Sound Design:} Native browser audio APIs provide instant, subtle UI feedback (clicks, correct/incorrect chimes, coin drops) to enhance cognitive retention and engagement without relying on heavy media files, making it ideal for low-bandwidth environments.
  \end{itemize}

\section{Creativity and Innovation}

\begin{keybox}
Unlike existing solutions (video courses, PDF booklets, in-person workshops), TruthQuest innovates in three simultaneous dimensions:
\end{keybox}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5cm, itemsep=8pt]
  \item \textbf{Learning by Discovery, Not by Instruction}\\
  The user does not receive a lecture: they face real content and make decisions. Mistakes become pedagogical opportunities, not punishments. This replicates the real social media environment where youth must decide in seconds if something is true or false.

  \item \textbf{Meaningful Gamification}\\
  It's not about "adding points to a course". The progression system (Digital Village, XP, streaks, badges) is designed with principles of \textit{Self-Determination Theory}: autonomy (the player chooses their path), competence (immediate feedback), and social relatedness (duels and community missions).

  \item \textbf{Real-Time Community Missions}\\
  Global challenges where the entire user community collaborates to "debunk" a real disinformation campaign. This creates collective awareness and transforms MIL from an individual exercise into a social movement.
\end{enumerate}

TruthQuest is designed to be sustainable without relying on continuous external funding, utilizing an ethical revenue model that never compromises the educational mission.
\end{keybox}

\midrule
\faIcon{palette}\;\textbf{Cosmetics} & Optional in-app purchases: village decorations (Lighthouse of Truth, Central Library), avatars, and visual effects. Purely aesthetic. \\[6pt]
\faIcon{handshake}\;\textbf{Alliances} & Sponsorships from NGOs, educational foundations, and verification agencies to fund in-app campaigns or content. \\[6pt]
